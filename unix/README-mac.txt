AMBER WRITER FOR THE MAC

To install, drag Amber Writer onto the Applications folder.

The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check Amber Writer for malicious
software, because Amber Writer is a free program from a small developer and has not
been sent to Apple for checking. To open it anyway:

  1. Double-click Amber Writer in Applications, then click Done.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to "Amber Writer was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway again and type your password.

(On macOS 14 Sonoma and older you can instead Control-click Amber Writer, choose Open,
and click Open.) After that it opens normally.

Keys on a Mac: Cmd does what Ctrl does on Windows (Cmd+S saves, Cmd+B bold), and
Option, or Control, does what Alt does (Option+N next typo). Tap Option on its own for
the menu, or hold Cmd or Option for a moment to see the keys. On a laptop keyboard,
hold fn for F1 to F12 (fn+F3 is help).

Documents are saved in your Documents folder unless you type a folder.
