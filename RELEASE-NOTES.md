Amber Writer is a distraction-free word processor that looks and feels like WordPerfect for DOS on an amber monochrome monitor. This is the first release for all three systems.

**Downloads**
- **Windows** (XP to 11): `AmberWriter-0.7.0-windows.zip`. Unzip and double-click `AmberWriter.exe`. Windows 10 and 11 may say it's from an unknown publisher: click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **Mac** (macOS 12 and later, Apple silicon and Intel): `AmberWriter-0.7.0-mac.dmg`. Drag Amber Writer to Applications. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't notarized by Apple: click *Done*, then go to *System Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway*. Cmd works like Ctrl, and Option works like Alt.
- **Linux** (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later): `AmberWriter-0.7.0-x86_64.AppImage`. Make it executable (file Properties, or `chmod +x`) and double-click it.

**What it does:** amber text on black, a 65-column page, WordPerfect 5.1 keys (F10 save, Shift+F10 retrieve, F7 exit, F3 help), a WordPerfect-style menu bar (tap Alt), bold, italics, underline and strikethrough, centered and flush right lines, a typewriter beep and wavy underline for typos, spell check, search and replace, printing, and word, character and page counts. It saves .rtf by default, and also .docx and .txt.
